Parallax Macro Reports cover economics, rates, currencies, and equity strategy for each market, with a synthesis layer that ties them into a single view. Delivered weekly, on the same framework every week.

<Note>
**What you get:** A weekly macro research service. Country reports with nine sections of analysis per market, plus a global cross-asset report that ties regional views into one coherent picture. The research draws on a library of roughly 200 million rows of market and macro data.
</Note>

## The Product

Weekly macro research in two forms:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Country Reports" icon="flag">
    Nine sections of analysis per market, covering macro data, central bank policy, fixed income, currency, sentiment, factors, and sector positioning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Global Cross-Asset Report" icon="globe">
    A top-down view that compares regions and asset classes on a single positioning framework, spanning equities, fixed income, currencies and commodities, and synthesizes the week's country-level findings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

AI research agents produce each country report in three stages: seven specialist analysts research and draft, a chief-investment-officer layer synthesizes their work into one thesis, and an editorial reviewer polishes the result. The research team sets the cross-asset pillar scores used in the analysis. The global cross-asset report draws on a broader analyst panel spanning asset classes and regions. The full process is described on the [methodology page](/macro/methodology).

## Why Teams Use It

**Consistent market coverage**: Macro Reports applies a common research framework across covered developed and emerging markets in Asia, Europe, and the Americas. The recurring section structure makes markets and successive reports easier to compare.

**An integrated market view**: the synthesis stage brings the specialist sections into one thesis and explains how their findings inform the allocation view.

**Geopolitics in the positioning call**: political and geopolitical developments are part of the research brief. Analysts consider structural changes beyond the weekly news cycle and their implications for tactical allocation.

**Weekly updates with continuity**: each report uses the latest available data and the record of prior views to explain what has changed and what continues to support the outlook.

**Support for the research team**: recurring macro coverage gives internal teams a common starting point for allocation discussions and client-specific analysis.

## What a Subscriber Receives

Every week:

- **Country reports** for covered markets, each with nine sections: market news and developments, key macro indicators, fixed income, currency, liquidity and sentiment, [tactical asset allocation](/glossary/tactical-asset-allocation), factor analysis, sector analysis, and sector positioning
- **A global cross-asset report** comparing equities, fixed income, and currencies across regions on a single scoring framework, with a global sentiment-regime read and a synthesized global view
- **Charts and exhibits** generated from the same data that drives the text, so visuals and narrative stay aligned
- **Continuity**: each report is aware of what the previous week's report said, flags what changed, and explains why

Reports are delivered as polished documents and in digital formats suitable for internal distribution. Programmatic access to report content is available through the [Macro Report API](/api-reference/macro/report). [Contact us](https://parallax.chicago.global/contact) to discuss enterprise access.

## Research Standards

Three practices guide report preparation:

- **Data discipline**: analysts receive dated quantitative inputs and are instructed to omit figures that lack supporting data
- **Automated review**: grading against editorial standards guides draft refinement and selection before synthesis and editorial review. See the [review process](/macro/methodology#automated-review-and-refinement)
- **Clear interpretation**: the editorial brief distinguishes observed data from forecasts and expresses forward-looking views in probabilistic terms
