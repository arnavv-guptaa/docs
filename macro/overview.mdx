Parallax Macro Reports cover what a full macro research department covers: economics, rates, currencies, equity strategy, and a chief-investment-officer layer that synthesizes it all. Delivered weekly, on the same framework every week.

<Note>
**What you get:** A weekly macro research service. Country reports with nine sections of analysis per market, plus a global cross-asset report that ties regional views into one coherent picture. Every report is rebuilt from a continuously refreshed base of roughly 600 million data points.
</Note>

## The Product

Weekly macro research in two forms:

    A top-down view that compares regions and asset classes on a single positioning framework, spanning equities, fixed income, currencies and commodities, and synthesizes the week's country-level findings.

Each country report is produced by purpose-built AI research agents rather than human staff, working in three stages: seven specialist analysts research and draft, a chief-investment-officer layer synthesizes their work into one thesis, and an editorial reviewer polishes the result. The cross-asset pillar scores are the exception and are set by the research team. The global cross-asset report draws on a broader analyst panel spanning asset classes and regions. The full process is described on the [methodology page](/macro/methodology).

## Why Teams Use It

**Coverage that does not depend on headcount**: a traditional research function assigns one analyst per region, and depth decays fast at the edges. Macro Reports applies one identical framework to every market it covers, every week, across developed and emerging markets in Asia, Europe, and the Americas. Same data disciplines, same section structure, same quality bar.

**One framework, applied the same way each week**: every report draws on a single authoritative source for key shared facts. The fixed income and currency sections are fed the same verified policy rate, so they do not drift apart on it. A single strategic layer then synthesizes the sections, so you read one house view rather than a stack of desk notes.

**Geopolitics in the positioning call**: political and geopolitical risk is a standing analytical dimension, not ad-hoc commentary when a crisis forces the topic. Analysts research structural developments on a deliberately longer window than the weekly news cycle, and the tactical allocation section must weigh political events in its call. Every covered market, every week.

**Freshness without fatigue**: the data layer refreshes continuously and every data-driven input is re-derived weekly. No stale template gets lightly edited. Each report is rebuilt from the ground up, then checked against the prior week so you get genuine week-over-week continuity rather than a fresh opinion each Monday.

**A defensible research budget**: broad macro coverage is the line item hardest to justify and hardest to drop. Macro Reports replaces or augments it, and frees your internal staff for client-specific work.

## What a Subscriber Receives

Every week:

- **Country reports** for covered markets, each with nine sections: market news and developments, key macro indicators, fixed income, currency, liquidity and sentiment, tactical asset allocation, factor analysis, sector analysis, and sector positioning
- **A global cross-asset report** comparing equities, fixed income, and currencies across regions on a single scoring framework, with a global sentiment-regime read and a synthesized global view
- **Charts and exhibits** generated from the same data that drives the text, so visuals and narrative stay aligned
- **Continuity**: each report is aware of what the previous week's report said, flags what changed, and explains why

Reports are delivered as polished documents and in digital formats suitable for internal distribution. Programmatic access to report content is available through the [Macro Report API](/api-reference/macro/report), [contact us](https://parallax.chicago.global/contact) to discuss enterprise access.

## How It Stays Honest

Macro Reports is built on discipline rather than flair:

- **No invented numbers**: if a data point is unavailable for a market, the report omits it. Analysts are prohibited from guessing
- **One source of truth per shared fact**: key facts such as the policy rate are fetched once, verified, and shared across every section that cites them
- **Quality gating**: every section draft is scored against editorial standards, and a draft that falls short is regenerated rather than published
- **Separation of observation and forecast**: the house style uses probabilistic language and forbids speculative timing calls
