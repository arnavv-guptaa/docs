The Valuation State is the pillar of the [Quantum Score](/glossary/quantum-score) that measures whether an asset is expensive or cheap, relative to its own history and relative to its peers.

## Beginner

### What It Means

Price alone tells you nothing about value. This pillar asks the question every investor should ask before the others: given what this asset has traded at historically, and given what comparable assets trade at now, is today's price high or low?

### Example

A stock market trading at valuations well below its own long-run norm and below comparable markets scores strongly on this pillar, a lot of pessimism is already in the price. The same market after a multi-year rerating, trading rich against both its history and its peers, scores weakly, much good news is already paid for.

### Why It Matters

Valuation is the slowest but most reliable force in markets. It says little about the next month and a great deal about the next several years. It also defines the margin of safety: cheap assets can absorb bad news; expensive ones cannot.

---

## Advanced

### How to Read It

A strong Valuation State reading means the asset is priced attractively against its history and its alternatives; a weak reading means much good news is already in the price.

### Common Misreadings

- **Treating cheapness as a buy signal**: cheap assets can stay cheap, and often deserve to be, a weak economy or deteriorating trend can justify the discount
- **Treating richness as a sell signal**: expensive markets can keep rerating for years when fundamentals and sentiment support them
- **Expecting short-horizon payoff**: valuation is a multi-year force; over weeks and months the other pillars dominate

### Reading It Against the Other Pillars

Valuation rarely triggers a view on its own, in the reports it typically acts as an amplifier or a brake on what the other three pillars are saying. Cheap plus improving fundamentals plus a firming trend is the strongest combination; expensive plus euphoric [sentiment](/glossary/psychological-wavelength) is the most fragile.
