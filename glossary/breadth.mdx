---
title: 'Breadth'
description: 'Understanding breadth - the share of stocks participating in a market move'
---

Breadth is the share of stocks participating in a market move, whether an advance is carried by most of the market or by a narrow handful of large names.

## Beginner

### What It Means

An index can rise for two very different reasons: because most of its stocks are rising, or because a few giant stocks are rising enough to drag the average up while the typical stock goes nowhere. Breadth tells the two apart.

### Example

Imagine an index at a new high while the median stock in it is below its own recent high. The advance is narrow: a handful of mega-caps are doing all the lifting. Now imagine the same index level reached with most stocks advancing together, same headline, far sturdier foundation.

### Why It Matters

Broad rallies are structurally sturdier than narrow ones. When an index rises on the strength of a few names while the median stock falls, the advance depends on very few things continuing to go right. Narrowing breadth is one of the most reliable early symptoms of a tiring trend; broadening participation is one of the healthiest confirmations of a new one.

---

## Advanced

### How to Read It

In the reports, breadth appears as a component of the [composite market-sentiment score](/glossary/composite-market-sentiment-score) and in the sector and market internals discussion. "Deteriorating breadth" during a rising market is a caution flag, the index is being carried by fewer shoulders. Improving breadth during a recovery is a sign the move has foundations.

### Common Misreadings

- **Treating narrow leadership as an immediate sell signal**: narrow markets can stay narrow for long stretches, breadth is a fragility measure, not a timer
- **Reading breadth without the index**: falling breadth in a falling market is ordinary; falling breadth in a rising market is the pattern worth attention, the divergence carries the information
- **Equating breadth with sector rotation**: rotation moves leadership between groups; breadth measures how many stocks participate at all, a market can rotate healthily or narrow unhealthily, and the sector analysis section distinguishes the two

### Reading It Against the Other Measures

Breadth is the participation check on [momentum](/glossary/momentum): a strong trend confirmed by broad participation deserves more trust than the same trend carried by a few names. When the reports pair a positive sentiment reading with a breadth-led warning, that pairing is deliberate.
