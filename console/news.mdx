News Intelligence scans company disclosures and news sources across major Asian and US equity markets, identifies potentially market-moving events, and assesses what each event means for the portfolio you actually hold. Scans run on a schedule or on demand. The output is a filtered stream of portfolio-relevant assessments.

For the methodology behind the pipeline, including how events are validated before they reach you, see **[News Intelligence Methodology](/methodology/news-intelligence)**.

## Portfolio-Aware Impact Assessment

Assessed events are mapped to actual portfolio holdings, connecting the event analysis to direct and indirect exposure.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Direct Holdings">
    **Position-level relevance**:
    - Every detected event is checked against the positions in your portfolio
    - Events touching nothing you hold do not interrupt you
    - Events touching your holdings arrive with the scenario analysis already attached

    **Impact in context**:
    - The same event carries different weight for different portfolios
    - Assessments reflect your actual exposure, so the output answers "what does this mean for me" rather than "what happened"
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="ETF Look-Through">
    **Indirect exposure captured**:
    - Holdings inside ETFs are evaluated at the constituent level
    - If an affected company sits inside an ETF you hold, the exposure is surfaced even though the name never appears on your position list
    - Direct and indirect exposure to the same event are assessed together

    **Why it matters**:
    - ETF constituents can create exposure beyond the directly held securities
    - Constituent-level reconciliation brings that exposure into the assessment
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Scenario Detail">
    **Explicit scenario structure**:
    - Bull, base, and bear cases for each event, each with an estimated price impact and an assigned probability
    - A probability-weighted view with visible scenario assumptions
    - The reasoning is shown, so you can challenge an assumption and still use the framework

    **Qualitative enrichment**:
    - Narrative context accompanies the numbers, explaining what the event is and why the scenarios take the shape they do
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
News Intelligence is fully automated and systematic: every item passes through the same detection, validation, and assessment sequence before it is surfaced. It is an input to your judgment, and the reasoning behind each assessment is visible so it can be challenged.
